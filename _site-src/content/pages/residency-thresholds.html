Track any tax residence rule

Pick a country and its test comes set: the threshold, the window, and whether days or nights count. Choose to stay below the line or to reach it. The common tests come ready to use, each with a plain-language guide.

  • US Substantial Presence Test31 days this year and 183 weighted across three years.Guide
  • United Kingdom Tax Residency183 midnights or more in a UK tax year, from 6 April.Guide
  • Ireland Tax Residency183 days in one year, or 280 across two years.Guide
  • Norway Tax ResidencyOver 183 days in 12 months, or over 270 in 36 months.Guide
  • Portugal Tax ResidencyMore than 183 nights in any 12 months.Guide
  • Denmark Overseas Work 42-Day Limit42 days in Denmark in any six months.Guide

How it works

Set it up once. After that, every tracker stays current with every day you spend in each country.

  1. 1

    Pick your countries

    Choose the preset for each country and its test comes set, or build your own. Decide whether you want to stay below the line or reach it.

    Create a tracker
  2. 2

    Add your trips

    Import past trips from your photos, Flighty, or a CSV file, or add them by hand. Add planned trips too, to see what they do to the count before you book.

  3. 3

    Hand over the record
