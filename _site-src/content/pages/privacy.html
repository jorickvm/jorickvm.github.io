AtlasDays App Privacy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

AtlasDays is built to never receive your trips or your photos, so we cannot sell, lose, or hand them over, not even when asked. There are no user accounts and no copy on our servers.

In short
  • Your travel history stays on your device. If you enable iCloud Sync, AtlasDays syncs it only through your private Apple iCloud account.
  • AtlasDays processes location, photo, and video data on your device. It does not upload your photos, videos, or raw location data.
  • The app has no advertising and no tracking. Anonymous usage statistics help improve AtlasDays. They never include your trips, dates, photos, or the places you have been.
  • If you came through an ad on the App Store, Apple’s privacy-friendly attribution tells the app which campaign and keyword, never who you are.

1. Storage and iCloud

AtlasDays stores your trips, trackers, notes, settings, and local photo and video references on your device. It has no server that receives this data.

On new installations, iCloud Sync is on by default when iCloud is available. It syncs trips and trackers through your private Apple iCloud account. You can turn sync off in AtlasDays Settings. Turning sync off stops new changes from syncing. Data already in your iCloud stays there until you delete it in your Apple Account’s iCloud storage settings.

Some preferences can also sync through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage.

To suggest a home country, AtlasDays may read your device region, preferred languages, and App Store country. This happens on your device only.

2. Location

Auto-Detect Trips is optional. It uses Apple’s significant-change location service, not continuous GPS tracking. Country and US state checks happen on your device. Raw coordinates are not stored or uploaded.

When enabled, AtlasDays keeps a local log of detected places and dates to add trips and manage ongoing trips.

You can turn Auto-Detect off in AtlasDays or revoke location access in iOS Settings.

3. Photos and Videos

Photo Import is optional. AtlasDays reads dates, locations, and limited metadata of the photos and videos on your device to suggest past trips. It adds nothing to your trip history until you confirm it.

AtlasDays does not upload your photos, videos, or their locations. It may store local identifiers and cache small previews on your device. These previews are not uploaded or synced. Originals remain in Apple Photos.

When you play a video in AtlasDays and it is stored only in iCloud Photos, the app asks Apple to download it to your device. That transfer is between your device and Apple.

Local photo and video identifiers may sync with a trip through iCloud, but the media files do not. If you favorite or unfavorite an item in AtlasDays, that action updates it in Apple Photos.

You can revoke Photos access in iOS Settings.

4. Notifications

Trip notifications and tracker alerts are generated and scheduled on your device. Notification permission is optional.

5. Imports, Exports, and Sharing

Imports, exports, backups, and PDF reports are processed locally. AtlasDays does not upload them. These files can contain your full travel history. Once you save or share one, it is outside the app, and protecting it is up to you. Data you deliberately share is handled by the app or service you choose.

6. Purchases and Support

Apple handles purchases and subscriptions. AtlasDays receives your entitlement status, not your payment details.

Sending feedback is optional. AtlasDays prepares an editable email that may include basic app, device, settings, permission, and usage information to help diagnose problems. You can review or remove this information before sending. If you send the email, its contents are used only for support and kept only as long as needed to resolve your request. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

7. Usage Statistics and Advertising

AtlasDays has no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites. It uses no advertising identifier and asks for no tracking permission.

To see how the app is used and where it can be better, AtlasDays sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, an analytics provider based in the European Union. There is no account and nothing that identifies you personally. The provider assigns an anonymous device identifier that is not connected to your name, email, or Apple Account, and cannot be used to contact you or follow you into other apps.

These statistics cover actions taken in the app, your settings, basic device and app information, and your approximate country. The settings include the home country you chose. Purchases and renewals are counted with the product, price, and store country. Counts, such as how many trips you have, are sent as ranges rather than exact numbers. They never include your trips, dates, notes, photos, videos, or coordinates.

Because the statistics are anonymous, AtlasDays keeps them as an archive. They cannot be tied back to you, so they cannot be looked up or deleted individually. The legal basis under the GDPR is legitimate interest: understanding how the app is used, with data that cannot identify you.

If you came to AtlasDays through an ad on the App Store, Apple can tell the app which ad campaign and keyword the download came from. This uses Apple’s privacy-friendly ad attribution: it identifies the ad, not you, needs no tracking permission, and is never connected to your trips or personal details.

8. Your Rights and Controls

You can delete travel data in Settings, revoke permissions in iOS Settings, and turn off iCloud Sync. Deleting app data does not cancel Apple purchases or subscriptions.

Your travel data lives on your device and in your iCloud, so you already hold it and can export it from the app. If you are in the EU, the UK, or a similar region, you also have the right to access, correct, delete, and port personal data we hold about you, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. The only personal data we may hold is what you send to support. Use the contact details in section 12 for any of this.

9. Children

AtlasDays is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets). It collects no personal data through the app, and sends no information that identifies a person.

10. Changes to This Policy

If the app’s privacy model changes, this page and its update date will change. For a material change, AtlasDays will say so in the app’s release notes.
