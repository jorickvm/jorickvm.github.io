AtlasDays / Help Center

Getting Started

AtlasDays records which country you were in on which day, then counts those days against the limits that matter to you. Trips live in the Timeline, the Dashboard shows what they add up to, and a tracker watches one specific rule.

What AtlasDays is for

Questions with a consequence attached: how many Schengen 90/180 days are still available, whether you are approaching a 183-day residency threshold, how long you have really been out of your home country this year.

AtlasDays keeps the record and counts the days. Interpreting an immigration or tax rule is still your responsibility.

Follow Set Up AtlasDays

On a new install, the Dashboard shows a Set Up AtlasDays card. Tap it for a checklist of five steps, each with a button that takes you where the step is done:

  1. Add Your Trips: import past trips or add them by hand.
  2. Add Where You Live: your home country, for tracker suggestions and your days abroad.
  3. Add a Tracker: the rule you want to watch.
  4. Detect Trips Automatically: new trips added as you cross borders.
  5. Turn On Notifications: trip updates and document reminders.

The card shows how far you are. Once you are done, or if you would rather not, touch and hold the card and choose Hide. You can show it again from Settings → Appearance. The rest of this article explains what each step gives you.

Fill your Timeline without typing

Photos cover the past. Auto-Detect covers what comes next.

Open Settings → Import → Photos and tap Find Trips in Photos. Photo Import reads geotagged photo metadata on the device and proposes trips for review, and nothing is saved until you confirm. Use Google Timeline Import when your Google Maps location history covers your travel, Flighty Import when your Flighty flight history does, or CSV Import when your history is already in a spreadsheet or another app. Import Trips compares the sources.

Then turn on Auto-Detect Trips in Settings → Auto-Detect Trips, so new travel is added to the Timeline automatically rather than entered by hand. Review the trip there or from its notification; you can edit or delete it at any time.

The Timeline holds the record

Every trip, in order, with the dates you gave it. Tap a trip to open its page, or tap + to add one by hand. An empty Timeline has no + yet: its Add your first trip card offers See Import Options and Add by Hand instead.

Older history does not need precise dates: a trip can be saved as Year Only when you only know the year, which keeps the country on your map without inventing days. Anything feeding a live limit needs its real dates.

The Dashboard shows what it adds up to

Your trackers, the Statistics card with your days per country, days abroad, and places visited, and the world map. The period on the Statistics card switches between all time, a single year, recent days, or your own dates, so the same record answers different questions.

Tell AtlasDays where you live. Onboarding does not ask. Open Settings → Profile and answer Where do you live now?, so that Days abroad knows which days are away and Add a Tracker can suggest the rules that may apply to you. If you have lived in more than one country, add each one with its dates in Where I've Lived.

Add your first tracker

A tracker is the reason to use AtlasDays rather than a travel diary. Tap + at the top right of the Dashboard and choose Add Tracker, or use the Add a Tracker step in Set Up AtlasDays. Tell it what you want to track, such as visas or tax, and pick a suggested rule, or browse every rule by region. A tracker then shows used days, remaining days, and how close you are. Create a Tracker covers the options.

A tracker is only as good as the trips behind it. Treat its total as provisional while the relevant trips are still approximate. Tracking one limit is free; more trackers and some rule types need Pro.

If the first totals look wrong

Check the selected period, where you live in your Profile, any open-ended trip still counting toward today, Transit trips, and whether a trip that matters was saved as Year Only.
