Photo Import

Photo Import scans the dates and locations in your photo library on the device, groups them into suggested trips, and saves nothing until you review and confirm. It is one of the ways to add past trips. To add photos to trips you already have, use Find Photos.

Start Photo Import

  1. Open Settings → Import → Photos. The screen is titled Find past trips in Photos.
  2. Tap Find Trips in Photos.
  3. When iOS asks, choose which photos AtlasDays may see. Full Access finds the most trips. With selected photos, AtlasDays looks only at the photos you shared.
  4. Keep AtlasDays open while it scans, then tap Review N Trips.

What AtlasDays examines

AtlasDays reads the timestamp and location of photos and videos: your whole library with Full Access, or only the photos you selected. Screenshots and screen recordings are skipped. The scan and country lookup run on your device, work offline, and nothing is uploaded to AtlasDays. Saved trips keep local references to the photos and small cached previews; the originals stay in Photos.

With selected photos, the scan offers Allow Full Access to find more trips. You can change the access later in iOS Settings.

Review every proposal

The review screen, Found in Photos, lists the proposed trips. Summary rows show counts of duplicates, merge candidates, overlaps, possible transits, and US states. Each trip can carry a pill such as Duplicate, Merge, Overlaps existing, Gap inferred, Near border, or Single photo. Import Trips explains Duplicate, Merge, and Overlap.

  • Tap a trip to change its country or dates, or clear its selection to leave it out. With three or more trips, Select All and Deselect All change the whole list.
  • A trip starts on the date of the first photo in a country and ends on the date of the last one, so the dates are estimates. A photo saved from a message or the web can add a country you never visited.
  • If your most recent photos are from the last few days, the latest trip is left ongoing, because you may still be there.
  • US states are proposed as separate trips with Pro. Without Pro, they import as plain United States trips.

Tap Import, then confirm to save the selected trips to Timeline. About these suggestions, below the list, explains what to check before importing.

Photos are evidence, not proof. Short stops between two nearby trips may be marked Transit, which never counts toward day totals. Turn Transit off for any stop where you spent time in the country.

Handle gaps conservatively

Photos without location cannot support a suggestion, and long gaps between geotagged photos may produce boundaries that need manual correction. Edit the saved trip in Timeline when you have better evidence.

Add photos to trips you already have

Find Photos looks for photos and videos taken during trips that are already in your Timeline. It does not create trips. It appears in Import Trips once you have trips.

  1. Open Settings → Import → Find Photos.
  2. Tap Find Matching Photos. AtlasDays checks every trip that has exact dates and a mapped place, and skips international airspace and waters.
  3. When the scan ends, tap Review N Trips, check the matches under Photos to Add, and tap Add Photos.

If nothing new turns up, the result is No Photos Found. Find Photos needs Full Access; with selected photos, the Photo Access Needed alert points you to Settings. AtlasDays saves references to your photos, not copies, so a photo deleted from your library disappears from the trip too. To choose where photo previews show, see Appearance and Personalization.

If no trips are found

If Photo access needed appears, open iOS Settings and allow AtlasDays to see selected photos or your whole library. If the scan finishes with No trips found in your photos, check that location was on when the photos were taken; trips already in Timeline are flagged as duplicates rather than proposed again.
