Appearance and Personalization

Appearance settings change how AtlasDays looks and behaves on screen: theme, accent color, background wash, app icon, photo previews, and animations. They never change trips, counted days, or tracker rules.

Choose a theme

Open Settings → Appearance. Under Theme, choose System to follow the iOS setting, or Light or Dark to fix it. Every choice on this screen applies immediately, with no Save step.

Personalize AtlasDays

Under Personalization, on the same screen:

  • The row of color swatches sets the accent color: active controls, progress bars, and the visited places on the map. Every color is free except Gold, which comes with AtlasDays Pro.
  • The slider below it sets the background wash, how much color tints the screen backgrounds, in four steps from off to bold. It starts at the third step, Medium.
  • App Icon appears for AtlasDays Pro users and lets you choose an alternative icon.

Photo previews

Under Photo Previews, choose where photos from your library show:

  • Timeline shows a row of photos on trip cards. It is off unless you turn it on; cards still show how many photos a trip has.
  • Trip Details shows the photos on a trip's own page. It is on unless you turn it off.

AtlasDays keeps small copies of the previews on this device only, and the originals stay in Photos.

Adjust the rest of Appearance

  • Animations (under Dashboard) plays the map reveal and count-up animations on the Dashboard and tracker cards. Turn it off for a static Dashboard.
  • Set Up AtlasDays opens the getting-started checklist, with how many of its steps are done. From there you can show or hide its card on the Dashboard. See Getting Started.
  • Show UN labels (under Countries) marks United Nations members and observer states in country pickers.

Widgets and exports

AtlasDays prepares widget map snapshots for light and dark appearances, and iOS chooses which one appears with your Home Screen. Exports and share cards have their own options.

If an app icon does not update immediately

Return to the Home Screen and allow iOS a moment to refresh it. Reopen AtlasDays if the old icon remains.
