AtlasDays Pro

AtlasDays is fully usable for free, including tracking one limit. Pro adds unlimited trackers, Smart Alerts, US state tracking, Spreadsheet and PDF exports, and larger Tracker widgets.

Included for free

  • Trip history, Timeline, Calendar, Dashboard, and the visited-places map
  • Tracking one limit, such as the Schengen 90/180 rule
  • Every import source: Photos, Flighty, and CSV
  • AtlasDays Backup, to export and restore your trips, trackers, and settings
  • Optional iCloud Sync
  • Appearance and personalization: theme, accent color, and background wash
  • The Small Tracker widget and all World Map widgets

Pro unlocks

  • Unlimited trackers
  • US state tracking
  • Smart Alerts on trackers
  • Spreadsheet and PDF exports
  • Medium and Large Tracker widgets

Photo, Flighty, and CSV imports still work without Pro. A US state that an import proposes is saved as a plain United States trip.

Upgrade, restore, and redeem

Tap Upgrade to Pro at the top of Settings, or tap any locked Pro feature. The plans shown are quarterly, annual, and lifetime, and the purchase completes through Apple.

If you already bought Pro with the same Apple Account, tap Restore on the upgrade screen. If you have an offer code, tap Redeem. Once Pro is active, the AtlasDays Pro row under About in Settings shows your plan.

If Pro is not recognized

Confirm you are signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase, check your connection, reopen AtlasDays, and tap Restore again. Apple manages billing and the purchase record.

Before purchasing

The upgrade screen is the authoritative price and plan list, because pricing varies by storefront and can change over time.
