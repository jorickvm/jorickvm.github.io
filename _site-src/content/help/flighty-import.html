Flighty Import

Export your flight history from Flighty as CSV, select it in AtlasDays, and review the proposed trips before saving.

Export from Flighty

Export your flight history from Flighty as CSV and save it somewhere the iOS Files picker can reach, such as iCloud Drive or On My iPhone.

Import into AtlasDays

  1. Open Settings → Import → Flighty.
  2. Tap Select Flighty CSV and choose the exported file.
  3. Wait for AtlasDays to build its preview.
  4. Review the proposed stays, Transit records, overnight cases, duplicates, and overlaps.
  5. Save only the rows you want in Timeline.

How flights become trips

Arrival and departure airports provide evidence for a stay, while connections may become Transit. Overnight and closely connected flights can require judgment, so compare the preview with your actual entry into each country.

Review US states

When airport information supports a US state, AtlasDays can propose it. Confirm the state before saving if you rely on state-specific tracking.

If the file is rejected

Create a fresh Flighty CSV export and select that original file. A spreadsheet that has resaved or rearranged the export may no longer match Flighty’s format.
