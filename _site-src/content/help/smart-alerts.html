Smart Alerts

Smart Alerts notify you as a tracker approaches or reaches its limit or target. They are set per tracker, look ahead using your recorded and planned trips, and need notifications to be on in AtlasDays and in iOS.

Turn on alerts

  1. Open the tracker's detail screen and tap the bell at the top right. The bell is filled while alerts for that tracker will be delivered.
  2. Turn on the Alerts toggle, and allow notifications when iOS asks.
  3. Set the Time alerts are delivered. The default is 9:00.
  4. Under Alert when, tap + to add a milestone. Tap a milestone to change its number, or to remove it with the trash icon.
  5. Tap Done. Changes made from the bell are saved when you close the screen.

The same screen opens from the Alerts row in the tracker's editor, and from Settings → Notifications. In the editor, alert changes are stored with the tracker when you tap Save, so Cancel discards them. The Alerts row and the Notifications screen show Off or the number of active alerts.

Milestones

On a Stay Below tracker a milestone reads N days remaining, and Limit reached is the final one. On a Reach Target tracker it reads N days to go, ending with Target reached. New trackers start with a set based on the limit; a Reach Target tracker starts with the finish line alone.

Alerts look ahead

AtlasDays projects the tracker about a year forward from your recorded trips, an ongoing trip, and planned trips. It schedules each alert for the date the milestone is expected to be reached, delivered at your chosen time. An alert can therefore arrive before the total has crossed the milestone, and a change to a trip or to the tracker’s rule moves the date. For a tracker with two limits, alerts follow whichever limit is nearest.

Notification settings

Settings → Notifications controls everything AtlasDays sends. Turning on the Notifications switch asks iOS for permission and turns on every kind. Then each kind has its own switch:

  • New country detections, when Auto-Detect adds a trip. It needs Auto-Detect Trips to be on.
  • Expiring documents, a reminder before a document in your Profile expires.
  • Tracker alerts, the Smart Alerts on this page. Below the switch, every tracker is listed with its alerts; tap one to change them.

With the Notifications switch off, AtlasDays sends nothing, whatever each tracker is set to. If you refused permission when iOS asked, AtlasDays shows Notifications Denied with an Open Settings button. You can also open iOS Settings → Notifications → AtlasDays yourself.

Tracker alerts require AtlasDays Pro. Without Pro you can still edit the milestone list, but the Alerts toggle shows the upgrade options and the Tracker alerts switch reads Alerts require AtlasDays Pro. The tracker itself works without alerts. New country detections and document reminders are free.

If an alert did not appear

Check AtlasDays notification permission in iOS, and that Notifications and Tracker alerts are on in Settings → Notifications. Confirm the Alerts toggle is on for that tracker, and check the delivery Time. Then compare the milestone with the tracker’s forecast: the alert is scheduled for the date the milestone is expected, which may still be ahead.
