Export and Reports

Open Settings → Export, choose a Spreadsheet or a PDF Report, set its period and options, preview it, and share the file. PDF reports are also kept in your Profile. To keep a restorable copy of everything, use AtlasDays Backup instead.

Choose an export

  • AtlasDays Backup saves trips, trackers, your Profile, and settings in one file that only AtlasDays opens. It is free. See Back Up and Restore.
  • Spreadsheet creates an Excel or CSV table for analysis and record keeping.
  • PDF Report creates a formatted report for paperwork, advisers, or your own files.

Exports are created on your device and are never uploaded. The Export row in Settings stays unavailable until you have at least one trip.

Set the period and detail

The first time you open Spreadsheet or PDF Report on a device, a short introduction explains it; tap Continue. Both then start with a period: All Time, a Year (calendar year, or a fiscal year with its own start date), or a Range of recent days or custom dates. The row under the period shows how many trips match and opens the list behind that number. Tap a trip in that list to correct it before you export, or touch and hold it and choose View to open its page.

The Spreadsheet screen adds the columns to include, the file format (Excel or CSV), language, date format, and row order. The PDF screen adds the layout (by country or chronological), whether to count days or nights, options for purpose, notes, US states, a country chart, a signature line, and the paper size, and Select for Export, where you can leave out individual records. Decide what belongs in the file before you share it, because an export can carry more detail than the recipient needs.

Add personal details to a PDF

The PDF Report can carry your name, nationality, passport, date of birth, and custom fields. Choose Personal Details, fill in what you want, and turn on Show on Report. These details are stored only on this device, and they are not part of an AtlasDays Backup.

Preview, then share

Tap Export Spreadsheet or Create Report to open the preview, then use the share button to send the file. If a trip is wrong, correct it from the list of matching trips or in Timeline. If one is missing, add it in Timeline. Then export again. Creating or sharing an export never changes your trips, and the file is a separate copy.

Reports saved in your Profile

Every PDF you make with Create Report is saved under Reports in your Profile, and Saved to Profile confirms it. Open it there later to view or share it again, or tap Create Report in Profile to make a new one. Saved reports stay on this device: they are not synced and not part of a backup. To remove one, touch and hold it and choose Delete Report.

Spreadsheet and PDF exports require AtlasDays Pro. Without Pro, they open on their introduction with an Upgrade to Pro button. Keep exported files private, because they contain detailed travel history and are not protected by AtlasDays once shared.

If the file omits a trip

Check the export period first. For a PDF, also open Select for Export: records without exact dates, such as Year Only trips, are listed under Approximate Dates and cannot appear on the report.
