Import Trips

Open Settings → Import, or Import… in the Timeline's ⋯ menu, and choose the source that matches what you have. Photos, Flighty, Google Timeline, and CSV File show their trips for review first, and nothing from them is saved until you confirm.

Choose a source

The Import Trips screen lists every way to add past trips. Imports are free, and they are processed on your device.

  • Photos finds past trips from the dates and locations in your photo library. It is marked Recommended while you have no trips, because it is the quickest way to start. See Photo Import.
  • Flighty turns a Flighty flight-history export into trip suggestions. It covers flights only. See Flighty Import.
  • Google Timeline finds past trips in the location history you export from Google Maps. See Google Timeline Import.
  • CSV File bulk-adds trips from a spreadsheet, a booking record, or another travel app's export. See CSV Import and the CSV Format Reference.
  • AtlasDays Backup restores a backup file and replaces what is on the device. It is a restore, not a review. See Back Up and Restore.
  • Find Photos appears once you have trips. It adds photos from your library to the trips you already have and creates no trips. See Photo Import.
  • Add by Hand opens Add Trips, where you enter one trip or a whole run of them, one after another. See Add, Edit, or Delete a Trip.

Review before saving

Photos, Flighty, Google Timeline, and CSV each end on a review screen with one card per proposed trip. Tap a trip to correct it, or clear its selection to leave it out, then tap Import and confirm. Selecting a file or finishing a scan adds nothing to Timeline by itself. When the import is saved, Import Complete shows how many trips and countries it added, and Open Timeline takes you to them.

Cards can carry a small pill that compares the trip with what you already have. Every source uses the same rules:

  • Duplicate means an existing trip already covers the same place and dates. Duplicates cannot be selected, so they are never imported twice.
  • Merge means the trip shares at least one day with an existing trip in the same place. AtlasDays widens that trip to cover both, and combines their notes and purposes, instead of adding a second trip.
  • Overlap means the trip shares a night with a trip in another country or US state. Both trips are kept, so check the dates before you import it. See Resolve Overlapping Trips.

The individual articles explain the extra checks each source adds.

Remove an import you do not want

If an import brought in trips you would rather not keep, open Settings → Manage Imports. The row appears only while your trips include imported ones.

  1. Find the import. Each one is listed by the date and time it was made, with its number of trips and countries. Older imports without a recorded date appear as Legacy.
  2. Tap it to see its trips. Tap a trip to open it, or touch and hold it to edit or delete just that trip.
  3. Tap the trash icon, then Delete, to remove every trip from that import.

Only the trips from that import are removed. Trips you added by hand or that Auto-Detect added are not touched. When an import merged into a trip you already had, deleting the import leaves that trip as it is now, so correct its dates by hand if needed. To remove a single trip instead, see Delete Data or Start Over.

If a trip you expected is missing

Check whether it appeared with a Duplicate or Merge pill, which means AtlasDays already had a trip that covers it. Trips without exact dates do not add to day counts, as described in How Trip Dates Work.
