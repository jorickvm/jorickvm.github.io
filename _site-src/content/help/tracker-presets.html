Choose a Tracker Preset

Tap + on the Dashboard and choose Add Tracker. Tell AtlasDays what you want to track, then pick a rule under Suggested for you, find one under Browse by region, search with the magnifier, or start from Custom.

What do you want to track?

The screen opens with four goals. Tick every one that applies:

  • Visas: Schengen and other short-stay limits, such as Schengen 90/180.
  • Tax: days toward tax residency thresholds. United Kingdom Tax Residency is the Statutory Residence Test: it asks about your UK ties before it saves. US state residency rules are here too.
  • Residence: absence limits that keep a permit, green card, or permanent residence.
  • Citizenship: presence and absence requirements for citizenship applications.

Your goals decide which rules are suggested. Browse by region always lists every preset. Tap the heading to fold the goals away once you have chosen.

Suggested for you

With a goal ticked, AtlasDays suggests up to six rules that may apply to you, based on where you live, which country issued your passport, and your trips. A country you live in or hold a passport from that has no tax preset is offered as a custom tracker instead. These suggestions need both answers in your Profile: until then, a Get suggestions for you card takes you there.

Each goal you ticked that no suggestion answers gets its own custom tracker at the end of the list: Custom Visa or Entry Limit, Custom Tax Residence Tracker, Custom Residence Permit Tracker, or Custom Citizenship Tracker.

A suggestion is a starting point, not a finding that the rule applies to you. Schengen 90/180, for example, is not suggested to holders of an EU, EEA, or Swiss passport.

Browse by region

Browse by region lists every preset, whatever goals you ticked. Your Profile does not narrow it either. Each place has a card: Schengen Area, European Union, US States, and every country. A card shows how many presets the place has, and a country without presets shows no number. Tap a card to see its rules, and tap a rule to open it in the editor.

Presets are named for the rule and the place. The full list lives in the app and grows with updates, so browse it rather than relying on a written list.

Search

Tap the magnifier at the top right and type a rule, an acronym, or a country. Search also matches the rule text, so a number such as 183 works. The pills above the results, Visas, Tax, US States, Residence, and Citizenship, narrow them to one family. Typing a country name also offers Start a custom tracker for this country, and the results end with Custom. When no preset matches, the list says No presets matching followed by what you typed.

Custom

Every card under Browse by region ends with Custom, which opens the editor with that place already filled in: the Schengen countries, the EU countries, or the one country. A country without presets holds only this row. On US States the row is Custom State, where you pick the state.

To start with no places at all, tap Custom at the end of the search results. The custom trackers for one goal, such as Custom Visa or Entry Limit, are under Suggested for you and in search.

How a preset differs from Custom

A preset fills in the places, Goal, Limit, and Window for a published rule, names the tracker, and shows the rule in words under the rows with a Learn more link to the guide for that rule. Every value stays editable. Custom leaves them all to you.

Some presets are not a single number. A few counting rules have two limits, and many absence rules count days away rather than days present. Both are labelled in the rule text.

What is free

Tracking one limit is free. A second tracker, US state presets, and rules with two limits or a combined test need AtlasDays Pro, and carry a Pro badge in Browse by region. You can open and configure any preset without Pro: the editor's Save button then reads Pro and shows the upgrade options.

A preset is a starting point. It counts recorded days against a published rule, but it does not know your visa, nationality, or exemptions. Read the Learn more guide and check the official source. Create a Tracker covers the editor.

If a preset is missing or does not fit

Search for the country, or start from Custom and set the places, limit, and window yourself. To request a preset, contact support.
