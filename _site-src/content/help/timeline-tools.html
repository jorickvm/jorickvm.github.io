Search and Customize the Timeline

The ••• menu at the top left of the Timeline holds search, the switch between list and calendar, sorting, and colors. Tap the title to jump to a year or month.

Search

Open ••• and choose Search, then type. The search field takes the place of the title. Search matches the country name, a US state, a purpose such as Business, your notes, and years. Tap the X at the top right to close it.

Search works in the calendar too: matching trips are listed as cards, and tapping one moves the calendar to that month and opens the trip. When nothing matches, the Timeline says No trips matching followed by what you typed.

Jump to a year or month

Once your trips span more than one year, tap the title at the top of the Timeline. In the list, it shows the year you are looking at, and tapping it offers a row of years: pick one to scroll there. In the calendar, the title is the month on screen, and tapping it offers the years above a grid of months.

On iPad and an opened iPhone Duo, a row of pills above the list filters it instead: All Time and one pill per year. Tap a year to show only that year, or All Time to see everything again.

The ••• menu

What the menu offers depends on the view.

  • Resolve N overlaps appears only when trips overlap. See Resolve Overlapping Trips.
  • Search opens the search field.
  • Show Calendar or Show List switches views on iPhone. Swiping sideways across the Timeline does the same.
  • Sorting (list) toggles Newest first and Oldest first.
  • Trip Colors (calendar) colors trips By Continent or By Accent.
  • Import… opens Import Trips, where Find Photos adds photos to the trips you have.

On iPad and an opened iPhone Duo, the menu shows both Sorting and Trip Colors, and the button with two arrows beside ••• swaps the list and the calendar.

Photo previews on trip cards

Trip cards show no photo previews unless you turn them on: open Settings → Appearance and, under Photo Previews, turn on Timeline. A card still shows how many photos its trip has. Trip Details controls the photos on a trip's own page, and is on unless you turn it off. See Appearance and Personalization.

A card at the top of the list

Auto-detect is paused appears above your trips when Auto-Detect Trips is on but location access is no longer set to Always. Open Settings takes you to iOS Settings, and the X dismisses the card.

If a trip seems to be missing

Close the search field. On iPad or an opened iPhone Duo, choose All Time in the year pills. The calendar places only trips with exact dates; Year Only trips appear in the list.
