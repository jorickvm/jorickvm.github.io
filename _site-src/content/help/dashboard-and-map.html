Understanding the Dashboard and Map

The Dashboard brings together your trackers, a Statistics card, and the map. Everything on it is worked out from your Timeline, so a total that looks wrong is fixed by editing the trips behind it.

What the Dashboard shows

  • + at the top right opens Add Trip and Add Tracker.
  • On iPhone, the top row shows the AtlasDays name, or today's date when Today's Date as Title is on in Settings → Appearance.
  • Set Up AtlasDays appears on a new install. It opens a short checklist for getting started; see Getting Started.
  • Every tracker has its own card. Tap a tracker card to open its detail.
  • The Statistics card shows your country days, days abroad, and places visited for the selected period. Tap the period on the card to choose another, tap a figure to open its full list, or tap the card's title to open the Statistics screen.
  • The map highlights the places you have visited in the same period.

The map and totals answer different questions

The map highlights every visited place that is not Transit, including places with no dates: a Year Only trip appears under All Time and for the year it names. Those trips create no day totals. The map can therefore show more places than the Visited statistic, which counts only your Country Count list.

Tap the map or pinch out to open the full-screen map. Drag to rotate the globe, pinch to zoom, and tap a country for its trips and days.

United States detail

A country-level United States trip highlights the US and contributes to US totals. State-level views need Pro and a state on the trip; an untagged US trip cannot be assigned to a state automatically.

Arrange the Dashboard

Touch and hold a card for its options. A tracker card offers Edit, Reorder, and Delete. The Statistics card offers View and Reorder, and the Set Up AtlasDays card View, Reorder, and Hide. Reorder lists the cards; drag them into the order you want.

On a wide iPad screen and an opened iPhone Duo held sideways, the cards sit in two columns: Statistics and the map on the left, Set Up AtlasDays and the trackers on the right.

To share your statistics as an image, use Share on the Statistics screen.

If a Dashboard card is surprising

Check the selected period first. Then open the Timeline and inspect the period's dated, Year Only, open-ended, and Transit trips, and verify Where I've Lived, Country Count, and any missing US state tags.
