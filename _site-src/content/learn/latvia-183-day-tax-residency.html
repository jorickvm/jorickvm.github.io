Latvia’s 183-Day Tax Residency Rule

Latvia treats you as resident at 183 days in any 12 months beginning or ending in the tax year, or through a declared Latvian place of residence.

Last verified: October 2026

You can be a Latvian tax resident through either a declared place of residence in Latvia or 183 days there in any 12-month period beginning or ending in the tax year. Staying below 183 days does not remove residence under the declared-address route.

Threshold
183 days or more
Counting window
Any 12 months beginning or ending in the tax year
Alternative route
A declared place of residence in Latvia
Tax year
Calendar year
Legal basis
Law on Taxes and Fees, Section 14(2) and (3)

The rule

Section 14(2) of the Law on Taxes and Fees sets the individual residence tests. Exactly 183 days meets the presence test. The period can cross 31 December, so counting each calendar year separately can miss it.

The law also treats a Latvian citizen employed abroad by the Latvian government as resident. A declared Latvian address is another independent route; a short stay does not override it.

How to count it

  1. Keep the arrival and departure dates for every stay in Latvia.
  2. Add the days of presence in each 12-month period beginning or ending in the relevant tax year.
  3. Compare the combined visits with 183 days, rather than resetting every January.

Section 14 states days of presence but does not itself spell out partial-day treatment. Keep both travel dates in your records and confirm their treatment with the State Revenue Service if those dates decide your status.

Example. You have 100 days of presence in the autumn and another 83 the following spring. If all 183 fall within one qualifying 12-month period, the day-count test is met even though neither calendar year alone reaches it.

Beyond the day count

Section 14(3) governs when residence begins and ends under the presence route. For someone not resident in the preceding year, residence starts from the first arrival in Latvia in the tax year. The departure provision has conditions, including closer ties abroad and not being resident in the following year. A tax treaty can also affect the result when two countries claim residence.

FAQ

Is Latvia’s 183-day test a calendar-year count?

No. It uses any 12-month period beginning or ending in the tax year, so visits across two calendar years can combine.

Does exactly 183 days qualify?

Yes. Section 14(2) says 183 days or more.

Can a declared address make me resident below 183 days?

Yes. A declared place of residence in Latvia is a separate domestic residence test. Any applicable treaty must also be considered.
