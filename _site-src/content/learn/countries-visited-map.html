What counts as a country here

The total uses the most common answer: 195 countries, which is the 193 member states of the United Nations plus the Holy See and the State of Palestine. Turn on territories to also mark places such as Greenland, Puerto Rico, Hong Kong and Antarctica. They are counted beside the 195, not inside it. How many countries are there in the world compares this with the Olympic, FIFA and Travelers' Century Club lists.

What counts as a visit

That is your call, and travelers disagree. Most people count a country once they have passed immigration and spent time there. Some count any ground they have stood on, airports included. Pick one standard and use it for every country. Does a layover count as visiting a country walks through the common rules.

FAQ

How many countries are there?

195 by the most common count: 193 UN member states and 2 observer states. Other lists run from 193 to over 300 because they count territories, sporting bodies or regions.

Is my selection saved or sent anywhere?

No. It stays in this page and is gone when you close it. If you choose to continue in AtlasDays, the countries travel in the link you open, straight to the app on your device.

Can I mark territories such as Hong Kong or Greenland?

Yes. Turn on "Show territories and other places", or select the place on the map. Territories are shown next to your country total.

How do I keep my map?

Select your countries and choose Track this in AtlasDays. The app keeps your visited map on your iPhone or iPad and adds to it as you travel.
