How the calculator counts

  • Two views of the same count. Excluded days shows how much of your allowance you have used: 35 days in a 365-day period, or 36 in a 366-day period. Qualifying days abroad shows your progress toward 330 full days. Switch between them with Show.
  • 12 consecutive months. The period ends on the date you choose. Move that date to check a different period.
  • 330 full days. A qualifying day is spent entirely in a foreign country or countries, from midnight to midnight.
  • Excluded places. Recorded time in the US, Puerto Rico, Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands, the US Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Antarctica and international waters does not count.
  • Travel days. The calculator also excludes the day before and after each recorded stay in an excluded place. Add foreign stays when their end or start date shows that a crossing happened on the same day. Overlapping excluded days count once.

The travel-day assumptions match the AtlasDays app. Dates alone cannot describe every flight or connection, so check the result against your full travel record. Read the FEIE physical presence guide for the IRS rules, travel examples and other requirements.

FAQ

Does reaching 330 days qualify me for the exclusion?

The day count is only one requirement. You also need a tax home in a foreign country and eligible foreign earned income. This calculator does not determine your eligibility or calculate an exclusion amount.

Does the period have to be January to December?

No. It can begin on any day and runs for 12 consecutive months. Choose its final day in Count up to.

Do I need a complete travel record?

Yes. Every unmarked day is treated as a full day abroad. Missing US stays, excluded time or travel days can overstate the total. A blank calendar is not evidence that you meet the test.

Do my dates leave the browser?

The calculation runs in your browser. Your dates are not sent to analytics or saved by the website, and reloading clears them. Choose Export or open in AtlasDays to transfer your stays to the app, or download a CSV to keep a copy.
