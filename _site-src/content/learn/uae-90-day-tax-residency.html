UAE 90-Day Tax Residency: Who Qualifies?

The UAE’s 90-day tax-residence route combines physical presence with residence rights and a home, employment or business. Ninety days alone is not enough.

Last verified: 19 November 2026

Ninety days is only one requirement. Check residence rights and the home, employment or business condition before relying on this route.

Presence
At least 90 days
Window

The three requirements

You need at least 90 days of physical presence within the relevant 12 consecutive months. You must also be a UAE national, a national of another Gulf Cooperation Council member state, or hold a valid UAE residence permit. In addition, you need a permanent place of residence in the UAE, employment there or a business there. A tourist entry permission is not the residence permit required by this route.

A permanent home need not be owned

The home must be continuously available with a stable right of occupation. It can be rented or otherwise made available to you. A room booked only for occasional visits does not establish that condition merely because you have used it several times.

Count the window, then check the conditions

Add all days with any physical presence, including arrival and departure days, inside the chosen 12-calendar-month period. Deduplicate overlapping records. The days need not be consecutive. For example, three separate visits of 30 qualifying days reach 90, but the residence-right and home, work or business requirements still need to be satisfied.

What the total cannot establish

The FTA may disregard days caused by qualifying exceptional circumstances. Keep evidence of your travel and the other conditions. Domestic residence does not automatically settle eligibility under a particular tax treaty or guarantee a tax residency certificate for treaty purposes. The separate 183-day route does not have the same additional conditions.

FAQ

Does a tourist visa qualify for the 90-day route?

Not by itself. This route requires UAE or GCC nationality or a qualifying UAE residence permit, as well as the home, employment or business condition.

Do arrival and departure days count?

Yes. Any part of a calendar day of physical presence counts under the UAE rules.

Is the window January to December?

It can be, but it is not limited to a calendar year. The test uses a relevant period of 12 consecutive calendar months.
