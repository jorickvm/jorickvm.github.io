Argentina Tourist Stays: 90 Days or Three Months?

Check the permission granted at entry. Argentina’s general tourist guidance uses three months, while specified visitor routes grant 90 days. The two can end on different dates.

Last verified: 19 November 2026

Use the departure date authorised for your own admission. A three-month permission and a 90-day permission can end on different dates.

Why both descriptions appear

Migraciones describes tourists as visitors entering for rest or recreation with an authorised stay of up to three months, extendable for a similar period. Other government guidance describes 90-day entry for specified visitors. A calendar-month period and a fixed number of days are not universally interchangeable.

Use the admission record

Check the date or duration recorded by immigration for your own entry, including any nationality or visa conditions. If your permission is for 90 days, maintain a 90-day stay record. If it is expressed in months or gives a departure deadline, use that deadline rather than assuming the same day count.

Extensions do not happen automatically

Migraciones requires an extension request within the applicable period before a temporary visitor permission expires. Keep the approved extension and update the departure deadline only when it has been granted. Do not assume that leaving and returning guarantees a new admission.

A practical record

Keep your entry date, authorised departure date, actual departure and any extension approval together. For example, an entry permission with a specific June departure date still controls even if a generic calculator produces a later date. The travel record supports checking the permission; it does not replace it.

A concrete example of why the wording matters

Illustrative calculation: if 1 July 2026 is day one of a granted 90-day stay, day 90 is 28 September. A permission that instead gives an explicit departure date of 30 September is a different input. Enter that official date; do not replace it with the result for a different allowance.

The phrase “three months” does not tell a date calculator whether the legal final day is an anniversary or the day before it. Where the permission supplies an end date, that removes the need to infer a convention.

Keep a permission record, not only a travel record

A trip log tells you when you arrived and left. To check a visitor stay, you also need the permission attached to that entry. Keep these together:

  • The arrival date and admission record.
  • The exact permitted duration or authorised departure date.
  • Any extension application and, separately, the decision granting it.
  • The revised departure deadline if the extension is approved.
  • Your planned and actual departure dates.

These are useful organising fields, not a substitute list of documents required by Migraciones. The key is to be able to trace a deadline back to the permission that created it.

FAQ

Is three months always 90 days?

No. Calendar months have different lengths. Follow the terms and end date of your own permission.

Can I extend a tourist stay?

The general guidance allows a similar additional period, subject to an application and approval. Check Migraciones for the procedure and timing.

Does a tourist allowance determine tax residency?

No. Permission to visit and tax residency answer different questions.
