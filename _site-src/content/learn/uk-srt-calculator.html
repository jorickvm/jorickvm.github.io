UK Statutory Residence Test Calculator

Count your UK days and check the sufficient ties threshold for a tax year. Answer four questions, then mark your stays, including planned trips.

Last verified: November 2026

This calculator covers UK midnights, sufficient ties and deemed daytime visits. It does not determine your full tax-residence status: the automatic work and home tests, transit and exceptional-circumstance exclusions, and split-year treatment are not evaluated.

Check your UK days

Choose the tax year and answer each question. Add UK stays from that year and the two previous tax years, plus your stays in other countries so the country tie can be checked.

How the calculator counts

  • UK tax year. Each year runs from 6 April to 5 April. Arrival nights count; departure days normally do not.
  • Your ties. Prior UK residence, family, accommodation and work determine which sufficient ties threshold applies. Check the definitions in the questions before answering.
  • History matters. The 90-day tie comes from your recorded UK midnights in the two previous tax years. For a previous UK resident, the country tie compares UK midnights with each other country in the selected year.
  • Deemed days. If you were UK resident in any of the previous three tax years and have at least three ties, qualifying visits without a UK midnight count after the first 30. These can include departure days and same-day visits.

For the definitions, automatic tests and exceptions, read the UK Statutory Residence Test guide.

FAQ

Does staying below the limit make me nonresident?

Not necessarily. This is a day-count and sufficient ties check. A UK home or full-time UK work can make you resident through an automatic UK test, and other SRT provisions can change the answer.

Why add stays outside the UK?

The country tie depends on whether you spend at least as many midnights in the UK as in any other single country. Missing foreign stays can make that tie appear when it should not. This tie applies only if you were UK resident in at least one of the previous three tax years.

Can I try planned trips?

Yes. Future stays count in the selected tax year. The result uses all the dates and answers you have entered, including your recorded history.

Do my dates leave the browser?

The calculation runs in your browser. Your dates are not sent to analytics or saved by the website, and reloading clears them. Choose Export or open in AtlasDays to transfer your stays to the app, or download a CSV to keep a copy.
