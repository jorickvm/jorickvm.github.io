Turn Your Google Maps Timeline Into a Travel History

If Timeline was on, Google Maps has been recording where you were. Export it from your phone and AtlasDays turns it into trips, a map and day counts.

Last verified: November 2026

Google Maps Timeline is the most complete record of past travel most people have, but it only shows places, not days per country. Export it from the Maps app as one file, select that file in AtlasDays, and confirm the trips it finds. The import is free and the file never leaves your iPhone.

From Timeline to travel history

  1. Pick up the phone that has your Timeline. Timeline is stored on each phone, not online, so the export has to come from the device that recorded it.
  2. Export it. On an iPhone, open Google Maps, tap your profile picture, then Settings → Location & Privacy → Export Timeline data → Save to Files. On Android, open the phone's Settings app, then Location → Location services → Timeline → Export Timeline data.
  3. Get the file to your iPhone. From an iPhone it is already in Files. From Android, save it somewhere your iPhone can reach, such as iCloud Drive.
  4. Open Settings → Import → Google Timeline in AtlasDays and tap Select Timeline File.
  5. Review the trips it found. Change what is off, clear what you do not want, and tap Import. Nothing is saved before that.

What you get

Trips with dates
Every country you were in, from the first to the last day your phone recorded you there.
A map of your travels
Your visited countries on a world map and a 3D globe, with a count of countries and continents.
Day counts
Days per country and per year, and trackers for limits such as Schengen 90/180 or a 183-day tax-residency test.
A history that stays current
Add new trips by hand, or turn on Auto-Detect and AtlasDays adds a trip when you cross a border.

Why Timeline alone is not enough

Timeline shows a day at a time: the places you stopped and the routes between them. It has no total per country, no way to count against a visa or residency limit, and no view on a computer any more: Google says Timeline is not available in Maps on a computer. The export is one file of raw visits and routes, which is the part AtlasDays reads for you.

How much history is in there

That depends on your settings, so it is worth checking before you rely on it.

  • Timeline is off by default. If you never turned it on, there is nothing to export. Use your photos or your booking emails instead.
  • Auto-delete removes older data. It can be set to 3, 18 or 36 months. With it on, earlier travel is already gone and cannot be recovered.
  • The data lives on the phone. Resetting the phone or deleting Google Maps deletes Timeline from that device, unless you turned on Google's encrypted backup and import it again.
  • There are gaps. Days when the phone was off, or Timeline was paused, have no locations. AtlasDays fills short gaps by estimate, so compare those dates with where you actually were.

An older download from Google Takeout also works: AtlasDays reads the Records.json file in it.

Your location history stays on your iPhone

AtlasDays reads the file on your device and uploads nothing. It has no account and no server that receives your trips. The export itself holds every place Timeline recorded, so delete the file from Files once the trips are imported.

What to check in the review

  • Airport stops. A stop where every location is at an airport is listed as a possible transit and starts unselected. Select it only if you stayed airside, and it imports as Transit, which never counts toward day totals.
  • Overnight flights. A night in the air is listed as International Airspace and also starts unselected. Flying over a country never creates a stay there.
  • Your latest trip. If your most recent locations are from the last few days, that trip is left open, because you may still be there.
  • Trips you already have. A trip that repeats one you already have is marked Duplicate, so importing Timeline after a photo import does not double your history.

For a map of where you have been, the result is ready to use. If the dates will go on a visa or citizenship form, confirm the ones that matter against official entry and exit records.

FAQ

Can I still see my Google Maps Timeline on a computer?

No. Google says Timeline is not available in Maps on a computer. It is stored on your phone, and the export is made there.

How far back does Google Timeline go?

As far back as it was turned on, minus anything auto-delete has removed. Auto-delete can be set to 3, 18 or 36 months, so check that setting before you count on older trips.

Is Google Timeline Import free?

Yes. The import and unlimited trips are part of the free version of AtlasDays. Importing US states as separate trips needs Pro. Without it, they import as United States trips.

Does AtlasDays upload my location history?

No. The file is read on your iPhone and nothing is sent to AtlasDays.
