Greece's 183-Day Tax Residency Rule

Greece’s day test is more than 183 days in any 12 months. A qualifying private-visit exception, other residence tests and cross-year filing rules can change the result.

Last verified: 12 November 2026

In short: presence in Greece for more than 183 days, cumulatively, in any 12-month period can establish tax residence. Partial days, including arrival and departure days, count. The day criterion has an exception for qualifying visits exclusively for tourist, medical, therapeutic or similar private purposes lasting no more than 365 days, including brief absences abroad. Other residence criteria remain separate.

The rule

The day criterion adds presence across a 12-month period. Separate stays can combine even when they fall in different calendar years. It is not a fixed 1 January to 31 December count.

  • More than 183. Exactly 183 days does not meet this criterion; 184 days does, subject to the private-visit exception. A presence day counts however short it is.
  • The private-visit exception is conditional. It covers the stated tourist, medical, therapeutic or similar private purposes only while its requirements are met. The stay must not exceed 365 days, counting brief periods abroad too. It does not cancel residence established through another criterion.

How to count it

  1. List your Greece stays with exact arrival and departure dates.
  2. Count partial days, including arrival and departure. Check qualifying private-purpose periods separately before treating the raw presence total as the legal answer.
  3. Add the days in a 12-month period and slide that period across your record, including stays on both sides of 1 January.
  4. When the relevant total is more than 183 days, check the other residence criteria and the annual filing treatment, especially when the period crosses two years.

Example. You spend 100 days in Greece between September and December 2025, then 100 more between February and May 2026. Assume the private-visit exception does not apply.

The stays total 200 days inside the period from 1 September 2025 to 31 August 2026, so they meet the day criterion. For a person transferring tax residence whose day test is completed in 2026, AADE’s annual filing guidance points to a resident return for 2026, rather than automatically treating the 2025 arrival year as a resident filing year.

Backdating and the year of the tax return

Article 4 describes residence under the day criterion as applying from the first day of presence. That wording must be read alongside AADE’s annual filing guidance. Circular E.2064/2023, paragraph 8, says that when a person transferring tax residence completes the day period in the year after arrival, the resident return is for the completion year. For the arrival year, a non-resident return is filed if one is required. Other residence criteria can still change the arrival-year position.

Beyond the day count

A permanent or principal home, habitual abode, or centre of vital interests in Greece can establish residence independently of the day criterion. Special rules also cover some Greek public servants abroad. If two countries claim residence, check the applicable tax treaty rather than treating the physical-day total as a complete decision.

FAQ

How many days can you stay in Greece without becoming a tax resident?

There is no universal safe number. Up to 183 days stays below this day threshold, but another residence criterion can apply sooner. A qualifying private-visit exception can affect the day test even with a longer stay.

Is it 183 days or more than 183 days?

The statutory day criterion is more than 183 days, so exactly 183 is below it and 184 meets the numerical threshold. The private-visit exception and other residence criteria still matter.

Does crossing the threshold make the previous arrival year a resident filing year?

Not automatically. AADE’s cross-year guidance says that a person transferring tax residence who completes the period in the following year files as resident for that completion year, with a non-resident arrival-year return if required. Check any other residence criterion separately.
