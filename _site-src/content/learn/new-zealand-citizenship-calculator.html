How the calculator counts

  • Five years before you apply. The window ends the day before the application date you choose.
  • Both requirements matter. You need at least 1,350 days across the full period and at least 240 days in each of its five 12-month blocks.
  • Each day counts once. Arrival and departure days count as presence. Overlapping stays are merged for the total.
  • Qualifying presence only. Visitor and other temporary stays do not become qualifying days just because they are entered here. Check the residence-status conditions in the guide.

FAQ

Can a high total make up for a short year?

No. Reaching 1,350 days overall does not replace the requirement for 240 days in each of the five years.

Can I test a later application date?

Yes. Change the application date and add planned qualifying stays. The overall window and all five yearly blocks move with that date.

Do my dates leave the browser?

The calculation runs in your browser. Your dates are not sent to analytics or saved by the website. Reloading clears them. Choose Export or open in AtlasDays to keep a copy or continue in the app.
