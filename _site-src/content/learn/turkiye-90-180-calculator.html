How the calculator counts

  • Arrival and departure count. Each calendar day you are in Türkiye counts, including both travel dates. Overlapping stays count once.
  • A rolling window. The date you choose and the preceding 179 days make up the 180-day period. Days drop out as the window moves.
  • Planned stays. To check a planned trip, choose its last day. During a continuous stay, the rolling total cannot decrease.
  • No reset at the border. A short trip out does not give you a fresh 90 days. Earlier stays remain in the window until they age out.

FAQ

Does this mean I can stay for 90 days?

Not necessarily. This calculator checks the overall 90/180 ceiling. A shorter allowance for your nationality or visa still applies.

Can I check a future trip?

Yes. Mark its arrival and departure dates, then choose the final day in Count up to. Include earlier stays that fall within the same 180-day window.

Do my dates leave the browser?

The calculation runs in your browser. Your dates are not sent to analytics or saved by the website. Reloading clears them. Choose Export or open in AtlasDays to keep a copy or continue in the app.
