How the calculator counts

  • Choose your route. The standard route checks 450 whole days away over five years. The spouse or civil partner route checks 270 over three years.
  • The final year has its own limit. Both routes also check 90 days away in the final 12 months. Meeting one limit does not replace the other.
  • Whole days away. The dates you leave and return do not count as absence. Overlapping trips abroad are combined, so a change of destination does not create a day at home.
  • The application date sets the period. Changing it moves both windows. The Channel Islands and Isle of Man are included in the home area for this calculation.

FAQ

Which route should I choose?

Choose the spouse or civil partner route only if you are married to or in a civil partnership with a British citizen and are applying under that route. Otherwise use the standard route.

Does a result within both limits mean I qualify?

No. This checks absence totals only. The other residence, immigration-status and naturalisation conditions still apply, and any request for discretion needs separate consideration.

Do my dates leave the browser?

The calculation runs in your browser. Your dates are not sent to analytics or saved by the website. Reloading clears them. Choose Export or open in AtlasDays to keep a copy or continue in the app.
