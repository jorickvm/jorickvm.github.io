How the calculator counts

  • Any part of a day counts. The day you arrive and the day you leave are both days in the country, so 1 to 10 June is ten days. A travel day between two countries counts for both.
  • One country, one year. Pick the country in the tracker and the window above it: a calendar year, a tax year from the day it starts (6 April in the UK, 1 July in Australia, 1 April in New Zealand and India), or the last 12 months. Only days inside that window count.
  • Stay below, or reach 183. Most people want to stay under 183 days. If you need 183 days to become resident somewhere, set the goal to Reach target: the bar turns green once you get there.
  • Planned stays count too. Mark a trip you have not booked yet to see whether it would take you past 183.

What it does not check

183 days is where most residence tests start, not where they end:

  • Midnights. The UK counts the days you are there at midnight, so arrival and departure days do not always count.
  • Any 12 months. New Zealand counts 183 days in any 12-month period. Check the last 12 months and the tax years around your stays.
  • The exact threshold. Some countries need more than 183 days, and a few use another number.
  • Other ties. A home, family or your main economic interests can make you resident with fewer days, and the US Substantial Presence Test also weights days from the two previous years.

The rule itself is explained in 183-Day Tax Residency Rule: How the Count Works, and each country's version is in tax residency by country.

FAQ

Does the day I arrive count as a day in the country?

In this calculator, yes: any part of a day counts, so both the day you arrive and the day you leave are days in that country. Many countries count this way. The UK counts midnights instead, so check your country's rule.

Does spending 183 days in a country make me tax resident?

Often, but not always. Many countries treat 183 days in their tax year as residence, some use a different threshold or period, and other ties such as a home or family can make you resident with fewer days. The calculator shows the count, not the decision.

Is my data stored?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and does not send, log or save the dates you enter. Closing or reloading the page clears them. To keep them, tap Track this in AtlasDays.
