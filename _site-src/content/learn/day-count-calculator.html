A day record without a tax verdict

Just count starts with no limit. There is no 183-day default or safe/unsafe status. The number is simply the total for the selected place and period. A day on which you visit two countries counts in both countries, so totals across countries can exceed the number of dates in your calendar. The bar shows counted days as a share of the selected period.

Use a personal cap or target

Choose Stay below or Reach target and enter your own number. For example, a personal cap of 30 days can help plan a summer away from home. A target of 60 days can help you spend more time near family. Neither number becomes a legal threshold because it appears in a calculator.

Choose the period that answers your question

  • Calendar year: total the selected year, from 1 January to 31 December.
  • Custom year: choose the start date of a one-year period.
  • Last 12 months: choose a count-up-to date. The window moves with that date and uses calendar months, not a fixed 365-day approximation.

A worked example

A stay from 1 to 10 June contributes 10 days. Another from 8 to 12 June in the same country adds only 2 new days, for a total of 12. If you leave that country and arrive in another on 12 June, that date can appear in both countries’ records.

Keep the record

Download the stays as CSV or continue with them in AtlasDays. Your entered dates remain in the browser until you choose to export or transfer them, and are cleared when you reload.

FAQ

Does this decide tax residency?

No. It counts physical-presence dates and optional personal goals. It does not apply country-specific tax, visa or residence rules.

Are arrival and departure included?

Yes. Both dates count. A same-day visit counts as one day, and overlapping dates in the same place are deduplicated.
