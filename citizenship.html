About

Know how close you are to citizenship.

Naturalisation, settlement, and permanent residence come with presence minimums and absence limits, often over five years at once. AtlasDays counts them against your real trips, so you know where you stand long before you apply.

Get AtlasDays

Track any presence or absence rule

Pick a preset and the minimum, the limit, and the window come set. For any other country, set the requirement yourself. Each rule links to a plain-language guide.

How it works

Set it up once. After that, the count stays current with every day you spend in the country and every day away.

  1. 1

    Pick your rule

    Choose the preset for your country, or set the requirement yourself. Rules with two limits, like the UK's, are tracked as one.

    Create a tracker
  2. 2

    Rebuild the years behind you

    Applications look back five years or more. Import your trips from your photos, Flighty, or a CSV file, or add them by hand, and review every import before it is saved.

    Ways to add your trips
  3. 3

    See both limits at once

    Every tracker shows where you stand today and where your planned trips take you. When a rule has two limits, both stay on screen.

    Check a planned trip
