Before you download

Is AtlasDays free?

Yes. The core day tracking, trips, one tracker, and the map are free with no account required. AtlasDays Pro is an optional upgrade, available as a quarterly or annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, that adds unlimited trackers, Smart Alerts, exports, and more. See what's included in Pro.

Can it track Schengen 90/180 and custom limits?

Yes. AtlasDays supports rolling windows, per-stay limits, calendar-year counting, and custom trackers.

How the 90/180 rule works

Can I import and export travel history?

Yes. AtlasDays supports Photo Import from geotagged photo metadata, CSV import, Flighty flight-history import, CSV export, and PDF travel reports. Photo, CSV, and Flighty imports show a review step before trips are saved.

Import and export guides

Can I add older trips with incomplete dates?

Yes. AtlasDays supports exact trips, year-only entries, and unknown-date visits. Approximate history stays useful without turning into fake precision.

How trip dates work

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your trips, trackers, and map stay available on-device without an internet connection.

Are there Home Screen widgets?

Yes. AtlasDays includes small, medium, and large Home Screen widgets. Add a tracker widget to see days used, days remaining, and a forecast without opening the app, or add a world map widget. Multiple widgets can show different trackers side by side.

Home Screen widgets

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. AtlasDays helps you keep records and count days. Interpretation of immigration or tax rules is still your responsibility.

Is there an Android version?

No. AtlasDays is iOS only for now.
