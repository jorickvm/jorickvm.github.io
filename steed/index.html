Steed

A training journal for your Apple Health workouts.

Privacy

Steed has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It sends nothing to the developer or to any server of its own.

Apple Health

With your permission, Steed reads your workouts and their details from Apple Health: routes, heart rate, heart-rate zones, effort, steps and sleep. It shows them on your iPhone and keeps a copy there, so the journal opens quickly.

The only thing Steed saves to Apple Health is the effort you choose for a workout, and only when you choose it. Health data is never used for advertising, never shared, and never leaves your iPhone through Steed.

Your notes

Your notes, and settings such as workout colours, are stored on your iPhone.

  • Exports go wherever you send them, such as Files, AirDrop or a message.
  • Automatic backups, part of the optional Stable purchase, are written to Steed's folder in your own iCloud Drive, under your Apple Account. They hold your notes, workout colours, your light or dark choice and a record of imported notes. They hold no Health data.

Maps and purchases

Routes are drawn on Apple Maps, which loads the map of the area shown. Purchases are handled by Apple; Steed never sees your payment details.

Support

Email steed@atlasdays.app with a question, a bug or an idea. For a bug, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Steed version (Journal settings › Version) and the steps that caused it.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your workouts come back from Apple Health. For your notes, restore a backup: Journal settings › Backup and import › Restore journal backup, then choose a .steed file. With Stable, the latest one is in Files › iCloud Drive › Steed.

Missing workouts or details

Steed shows what Apple Health has and what you allow it to read. To change that, open Journal settings › Apple Health › Health access, then in Health your picture › Apps › Steed.
