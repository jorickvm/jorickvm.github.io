About

Know exactly how long you can stay.

Schengen 90/180, a single-entry visa, a visa-free stay, or the limit on your own visa. AtlasDays counts your real trips against it and tells you the date you have to leave by.

Track any visa

Choose the country, enter the days your visa allows, and pick how they count: per entry, per year, or in a rolling window. The common ones come ready to use, each with a plain-language guide.

How it works

Set it up once. After that, the count stays current with every day you travel, and with every trip you plan.

  1. 1

    Pick your rule

    Choose the preset for your visa, or set any limit yourself. The window and the counting method come already set.

    Set up a Schengen tracker
  2. 2

    Add your trips

    Import past trips from your photos, Flighty, or a CSV file, or add them by hand. Add planned trips too, to see what they do to the count before you book.

    Ways to add your trips
  3. 3

    Know before the border

    Every tracker turns orange as you get close and red at the limit. Smart Alerts, part of AtlasDays Pro, warn you while you can still change the booking.

    About Smart Alerts
