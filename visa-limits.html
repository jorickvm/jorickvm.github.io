Know how many days you have left before you book.

Schengen 90/180, single-entry visas, per-stay caps, and rolling windows. AtlasDays counts them against your real trips and tells you the date you have to leave by.

A rolling window never resets on a date you can circle.

Every day inside the zone changes the answer, and every day outside it slowly gives days back. The question is how many you will have used on the day you land, and on the day you plan to leave.

  1. Step 1

    Start from the rule, not a blank form.

    Pick Schengen 90/180, a single-entry visa, a per-stay cap, or a country's own entry limit. The window, the reset, and the counting method arrive already set. Set up a Schengen tracker

  2. Step 2

    Every limit on one screen.

    Schengen, per-stay caps, and your other trackers side by side, each with the days you have left. Add a planned trip and every count updates before you book. Plan a trip

  3. Step 3

    Know before you reach the limit.

    Trackers turn orange as you get close and red at the limit. Smart Alerts, part of AtlasDays Pro, warn you while you can still change the booking. About Smart Alerts
