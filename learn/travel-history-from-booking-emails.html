Turn Booking Emails Into Travel History in AtlasDays

Recover past trips from your inbox, review the dates, and import the confirmed stays into AtlasDays.

Last verified: November 2026

Search for transport and accommodation records, follow each booking through later changes, and record only the travel you can support. Keep the evidence and unresolved questions beside the dates. A booking confirms an arrangement, not that the trip happened.

Search for the whole journey

Search all relevant mailboxes for airline or hotel names, destinations and phrases such as “booking confirmation”, “boarding pass”, “itinerary”, “cancellation” and “flight changed”. Add the year or a date filter. Include rail, ferry and bus journeys; a flight-only history misses land and sea crossings.

Keep related messages together, including attachments. The date an email arrived is not the date of travel. Read the itinerary itself, and check whether a later message changes or cancels it.

Follow changes before creating a row

Suppose an original ticket shows arrival in Lisbon on 3 June 2026 and departure on 9 June. A later change moves the departure to 10 June. A cancelled hotel booking covers 3–6 June, while the final hotel invoice covers 3–9 June. If evidence confirms you actually took the revised flights, record Portugal from 3–10 June 2026. Keep the change message and confirmation of travel as support.

Do not create a second stay from the cancelled hotel, or treat its nights as a different journey. Hotel checkout is not necessarily departure from the country. If you cannot establish that a booked flight was taken, leave the travel dates unresolved.

Create one row per country stay

Use the free travel history CSV template. It has columns for country, state, arrival, departure, purpose and notes. Keep the English headers unchanged for import; write exact dates as YYYY-MM-DD. Split a multi-country journey into its actual stays, not one row for each email. Use the CSV file for a one-time import into AtlasDays, then keep your ongoing travel history in the app.

  • Use Notes to identify the supporting messages, changed bookings and remaining uncertainty. Keep sensitive documents in a separate private folder.
  • Distinguish the local arrival date from the departure date of an overnight flight.
  • Merge duplicate confirmations for the same stay. Resolve conflicting versions before counting days.
  • Keep approximate history approximate. Do not invent exact dates to fill empty cells.

Use an assistant only for extraction

You can organize the messages manually or ask an AI assistant to extract a draft. Remove passport details, payment information and unrelated personal text first. Anything you paste is handled by that service under its own privacy policy.

Extract a draft travel record from these messages. Follow later changes and cancellations. Separate bookings from evidence of travel actually completed. Use one row per country stay. Keep exact dates separate from uncertain dates, identify each source, and list unresolved questions. Do not infer missing arrivals or departures. Return a table for review before producing an import file.

Compare every proposed row with its sources. AtlasDays also provides a CSV conversion prompt and an import preview; the CSV Import guide explains the workflow. Import only after reviewing the countries, dates, duplicates and overlaps.

Fill the gaps with stronger records

Missing emails do not mean you did not travel. Check official entry and exit records, photos and other dated evidence. The full travel-history reconstruction guide explains how to combine those sources. Your imported travel history remains a record you prepared, not independent proof of every stay.

FAQ

Can a booking email prove I entered a country?

A booking shows an arrangement. Look for records of travel actually completed and, when available, official border records before treating it as an entry date.

Should I count hotel nights as days in the country?

No. Hotel nights describe accommodation. Your arrival, departure and the applicable counting method determine the country-day total.

Can an assistant read my whole inbox for this?

This method needs only the relevant records. Share the minimum text needed, review the service's privacy terms, and check the extracted dates yourself.

Official source

Apple's Mail search guide describes searching messages and narrowing by timeframe. The reconstruction method distinguishes booked travel from evidence of completed travel; it does not make a booking an official entry record.
