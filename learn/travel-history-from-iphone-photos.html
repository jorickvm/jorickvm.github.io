Turn Your iPhone Photos Into a Travel Map and Trip History

Your photo library already knows where you have been. AtlasDays reads it on your iPhone and turns it into trips, a map and day counts.

Last verified: November 2026

Almost every photo you take stores a date and a location. Photo Import in AtlasDays scans them on your device, groups them into trips by country, and shows you the list to confirm. It takes a few minutes, it is free, and no photo leaves your iPhone.

From photo library to travel history

  1. Get AtlasDays. It is free on the App Store, with no account to create.
  2. Open Settings → Import → Photos and tap Find Trips in Photos.
  3. Choose what AtlasDays may see. Full Access finds the most trips. You can also share selected photos only, and AtlasDays then looks at nothing else.
  4. Review the trips it found. Each one shows a country and dates. Change what is off, clear what you do not want, and tap Import.

That is the whole job. There is no need to open photos one by one or write down dates. The Photo Import walkthrough shows each screen.

What you get

A map of your travels
Every country you have visited, filled in on a world map and a 3D globe, with a count of countries and continents.
A Timeline
Your trips in order, with dates you can edit and the photos from each trip.
Day counts
Days per country and per year, and trackers for limits such as Schengen 90/180 or a 183-day tax-residency test.
A history that stays current
Add new trips by hand, or turn on Auto-Detect and AtlasDays adds a trip when you cross a border.

Your photos stay on your iPhone

AtlasDays reads two things from each photo and video: when it was taken and where. The scan and the country lookup run on your device and work offline. Nothing is uploaded, AtlasDays has no account, and the originals stay in Photos. Screenshots and screen recordings are skipped.

What to check in the review

A trip starts on the date of the first photo in a country and ends on the last one, which is close but not always exact. Four things are worth a look before you import.

  • First and last days. If you landed in the evening and took your first photo the next morning, move the start date back a day.
  • Photos that are not yours. A picture saved from a message or the web can carry a location you never visited. Clear that trip.
  • Short stops. A stop between two trips may be marked Transit, which never counts toward day totals. Turn Transit off if you spent time there.
  • Trips without photos. No photo with a location means no suggestion. Add those trips by hand, or recover them from your booking emails with AI.

For a map of where you have been, the suggestions are ready to use. If the dates will go on a visa or citizenship form, confirm the ones that matter against official entry and exit records.

FAQ

Is Photo Import free?

Yes. Photo Import and unlimited trips are part of the free version of AtlasDays. Proposing US states as separate trips needs Pro. Without it, they import as United States trips.

Does AtlasDays upload my photos?

No. The scan runs on your iPhone and reads only the date and location of each photo and video. Nothing is sent to AtlasDays.

What if my photos have no location?

Photos taken with location turned off, and most pictures from a separate camera, have no place stored, so they cannot suggest a trip. Add those trips by hand or from your booking emails.

How far back does it go?

As far as your library does. If your photos from 2012 are in Photos with their locations, the trips from 2012 are found.

Official sources

Apple explains how to see photo dates and locations and review location metadata. The AtlasDays steps are described in its Photo Import Help guide. A photo records a moment, not a verified border crossing.
