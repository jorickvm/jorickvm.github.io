Shift Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Shift does not collect or transmit personal data.

Data Collection

Shift does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party services that collect data.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your workout presets, workout history, settings, and active timer state are stored locally on your device. Local notifications and Live Activities are handled on-device by iOS. This information is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Retention and Deletion

Because the developer receives no data, there is no developer-held data to retain or delete. You can remove Shift’s locally stored data from your device by deleting the app.

Changes

If Shift’s data practices change, this policy and its update date will be revised before the changes take effect.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Shift support.
