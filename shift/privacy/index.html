Shift Privacy Policy

Shift does not collect or transmit personal data.

Data Collection

Shift does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party services that collect data.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your workouts, history, settings, and timer state stay on your device. Spoken cues are AI-generated and played locally.

Retention and Deletion

Because the developer receives no data, there is no developer-held data to retain or delete. You can remove Shift’s locally stored data from your device by deleting the app.

Contact and Support

For help with Shift, feedback, or questions about this privacy policy, contact shift@atlasdays.app.
