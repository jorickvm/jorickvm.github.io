Export and Reports

Open Settings → Export, choose the format that fits the purpose, configure its period and options, preview it, and share the finished file.

Choose an export

  • AtlasDays CSV is designed for backup or moving trip data back into AtlasDays.
  • Spreadsheet creates a configurable Excel or CSV file for analysis and record keeping.
  • PDF creates a readable travel report for review or sharing.

Configure the period and detail

Choose the date range, language, date format, and the columns or report options you want. Decide whether state, purpose, and notes belong in the file before you share it, because an export can carry more detail than the recipient needs.

Preview before sharing

The preview shows what the export will contain. If a trip is missing or needs correction, return to Timeline, edit the source record, and create the export again.

Exports do not change the app

Creating or sharing an export does not remove, mark, or alter trips. The exported file is a separate copy.

CSV, Spreadsheet, and PDF export require AtlasDays Pro. Keep exported files secure because they may contain detailed travel history.

If the file omits a trip

Check the export period and then inspect the trip in Timeline. Approximate trips may be represented differently from exact trips depending on the chosen format.
