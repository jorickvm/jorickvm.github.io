Understanding the Dashboard and Map

The Dashboard summarizes your Timeline for the selected period. The map answers where you have been; numeric cards answer how many qualifying days are recorded.

Start with the selected period

Use the period control at the top of the Dashboard before comparing a card with a trip or export. A calendar year, recent period, and all-time view can legitimately show different results.

Days Abroad uses Home Country

With a Home Country selected, Days Abroad reflects exact recorded days outside that country. With No fixed residence, it reflects exact recorded travel days without subtracting one home country.

The map and totals answer different questions

A non-Transit trip can highlight a country as visited even when it has no dates: a Year Only trip, or a visit imported without dates. Those trips do not create numeric day totals. Transit does neither.

United States detail

A country-level United States trip can highlight the US and contribute to US totals. State-level views require a state on the trip; an untagged US trip cannot be assigned to a state automatically.

Share cards follow the Dashboard

Review the period and visible result before sharing. You can choose the share-card appearance and language without changing the underlying trip record.

If a Dashboard card is surprising

Open the Timeline and inspect the period’s dated, Year Only, ongoing, and Transit trips. Then verify Home Country and any missing US state tags.
