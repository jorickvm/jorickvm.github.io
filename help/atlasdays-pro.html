AtlasDays Pro

AtlasDays is usable for free with one tracker. Pro unlocks advanced monitoring, exports, state-level records, and richer tracker widgets.

Included for free

  • Trip history, Timeline, Calendar, Dashboard, and visited-country map
  • One tracker
  • Photo, CSV, and Flighty import review
  • Small Tracker Status widget and all World Map widget sizes
  • Optional iCloud Sync

Pro unlocks

  • Unlimited trackers and Smart Alerts
  • US state tracking
  • AtlasDays CSV, Spreadsheet, and PDF exports
  • Medium and large Tracker Trend widgets with charts and forecasts
  • Additional personalization options, listed in full on the upgrade screen

A Pro feature you have not unlocked shows the same upgrade route where you meet it.

Purchase and restore

Open the AtlasDays Pro screen from Settings or a locked feature. Complete the purchase through Apple. If you already purchased with the same Apple Account, use Restore Purchases.

If Pro is not recognized

Confirm the App Store Apple Account, check connectivity, reopen AtlasDays, and try Restore Purchases. Apple manages billing and the purchase record.

Before purchasing

Use the current upgrade screen as the authoritative feature and price list, because availability and pricing can vary by storefront and change over time.
