Create a Tracker

Create a tracker after the trips it should count are in your Timeline with real dates, then verify the rule’s places, window, and limit before using its result.

Add a tracker

  1. Open the Dashboard and tap +.
  2. Choose a category: Residence, Schengen, Visa & Entry Limits, Travel Goal, US State, or Custom.
  3. Select a preset when one matches your need, or choose a custom setup.
  4. Review the name, countries or US states, time window, and limit.
  5. Save the tracker.

The category you pick decides which presets you are offered next.

Understand the window

  • Rolling window looks back a set number of calendar days from the selected date.
  • Calendar year uses January 1 through December 31.
  • Per entry evaluates a continuous stay rather than adding unrelated visits.

Want to track the Schengen 90/180 rule? Follow the dedicated walkthrough instead of building it by hand.

Review the places

A multi-country tracker counts qualifying days across its selected country set. A US state tracker needs state-tagged United States trips; country-only US records cannot feed a specific state.

Presets are starting configurations

A preset saves setup time. It does not change the rule you are actually subject to, so confirm it matches your nationality, permission, purpose, and current situation before relying on the number.

Free AtlasDays includes one tracker. AtlasDays Pro adds unlimited trackers and Smart Alerts.

If the new tracker has no days

Confirm that its places match your trips, and that the relevant trips have start and end dates rather than Year Only, and are not marked Transit.
