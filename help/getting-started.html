Getting Started

Start with three things: choose how AtlasDays should treat home, record the trips that matter now, and only then add a tracker.

1. Choose your home-country setup

Open Settings → Home Country. Choose the country that should be treated as home, or choose No fixed residence if you do not want any country treated as home.

This choice affects Days Abroad. It does not rewrite your trips or change a country-specific tracker.

2. Add the trips that matter now

  1. Open Timeline and tap +.
  2. Choose the country and, for a United States trip, the relevant state when known.
  3. Use Exact Dates for trips that should contribute to day totals.
  4. Save the trip and repeat for the period you currently need to monitor.

You do not need to reconstruct your entire travel life before AtlasDays becomes useful. Begin with the rolling window, calendar year, visa application, or residency question that matters today.

3. Create your first tracker

Open Trackers, tap +, and choose a preset or build a custom tracker. Check its countries, window, and limit before relying on its total.

Import older history when useful

Photo Import, Flighty Import, and CSV Import can accelerate setup. Every import includes a review step; nothing should be saved until the proposed trips look right.

A useful first record beats a complete approximate record. Exact trips that affect a live question should come before uncertain older visits.

If the first totals are unexpected

Check the selected Dashboard period, your Home Country setting, open-ended trips, Transit trips, and whether important trips use Exact Dates.
